# QR & Barcode Generator - Versatile Identification for Botble CMS

![QR Barcode Generator Banner](https://placehold.co/1200x400/34495e/ffffff?text=QR+and+Barcode+Generator)

**QR & Barcode Generator** is a powerful utility for Botble CMS that allows you to instantly generate high-quality QR codes and barcodes directly from your admin panel. Perfect for inventory management, product identification, or creating scannable marketing links, this plugin provides a clean and efficient workflow for all your scannable data needs.

---

## 💎 Premium Features

- **🔳 QR Code Generation:** Generate QR codes for URLs, text, email addresses, and more.
- **🏷️ Barcode Support:** Supports multiple barcode formats (EAN-13, Code 128, etc.) for product labeling.
- **🎨 Visual Customization:** Adjust colors, sizes, and margins to match your branding.
- **📥 Instant Export:** Download your generated codes in high-resolution image formats for print or digital use.
- **🛠️ Dedicated Admin Interface:** A streamlined standalone generator page within your admin panel.
- **⚡ Fast and Reliable:** Built on robust generation libraries for consistent and accurate results.

- **Botble Core:** Version `7.0.0` or higher.

## 🛠 Installation

### 1. Automatic Installation
1. Go to your **Admin Panel** -> **Plugins** -> **Add New Plugin**.
2. Search for **QR & Barcode Generator**.
3. Click **Install**, then **Activate**.

### 2. Manual Installation
1. Download the plugin from the marketplace.
2. Upload the `qr-barcode-generator` folder to `platform/plugins/`.
3. Go to **Admin Panel** > **Plugins** and click **Activate**.

---

## 📖 How to Use

1. Navigate to **Admin Panel** -> **Utilities** -> **QR & Barcode**.
2. Select whether you want to generate a **QR Code** or a **Barcode**.
3. Enter the data you wish to encode (e.g., a product URL or SKU).
4. Customize the visual settings (size, color).
5. Click **Generate** and then **Download** to save your image.
